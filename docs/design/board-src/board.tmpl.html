Grands Prix and tyres

A race is its Grand Prix and its flag, never "R21". Every list, card and picker leads with the flag and the Grand Prix name; the specimens above show the race card and a calendar list that way. Tyres are one Pirelli-style icon at every size: the black tyre, the compound's band near the rim, the letter in the band's colour, in one colour set shared by the app, the charts and the social cards. A stint is one strip summing to the race, with its words beside it.
