F1 DataStop design release, round 1, 10 Nov 2026

Three directions and six marks

Pick one direction and one mark, or mix: a mark from one list can wear another direction's colours. Each direction is shown in its own night and day, with the same three pieces: a race card, the standings and a lap chart. Figures are shaped like the real season; session times are sample data. Team colours, purple for fastest and tyre colours are the real ones and never change.

Marks, round 1
