# How the pages connect, 2026-11-10

The owner, 2026-11-10: "some parts of the whole f1datastop is disconnected, like i need to
click a lot of things that are displaced". This review maps every page's links, the
content stuck behind dialogs, the features that live in two or three places, and the
click paths for common tasks. Read from `web/src` on `main` at `7730106`; file
references are relative to `web/src/`. The proposed fix is decisions D19 to D24 in
[`../decisions.md`](../decisions.md), drawn on the board's "How it connects" section.

## The short version

The app has about 20 internal links, and almost none are on names. Driver names are
links in 2 of about 24 places, teams in 3 of about 17, Grands Prix in 2, sessions in
none. Every race click on Home and the Calendar opens `RaceDetailDialog`, which has no
URL, always opens on its Schedule tab, and is the only way into Race Analysis. Race
Analysis, the Pit Wall, the driver page and the team page link out to nothing. Each
page picks its season on its own, and five pages are locked to the current year.

## Content with no URL

`RaceDetailDialog` (`components/calendar/RaceDetailDialog.tsx`) opens from 7 places (5 on
Home, 2 on the Calendar). It holds the schedule, sprint qualifying, sprint, qualifying and
the full race classification. Its tab is local state and resets to Schedule, so the "last
race" chip lands on the schedule, not the result. Nothing in it can be shared, reached with
Back, or linked to.

## Navigation and season

- Phone tabs: Home, Calendar, Standings, Analysis, Drivers. The Pit Wall and Teams are only in the menu drawer; on a team or circuit page no tab lights up.
- The Analysis tab opens round 1 of this year, not the latest race. The Pit Wall opens empty.
- The Pit Wall has no inbound links apart from the navigation. There is no circuits index and no search.
- Season: the Calendar keeps it in `?year`, Race Analysis in the path, the Pit Wall in `?year`; Drivers and Constructors in component state (lost on navigation); Standings, the driver, team and circuit pages and Home use the current year. The season never carries from one page to the next.

## Ranked: what costs the most

1. Driver names are almost never links.
2. Race clicks open a dialog with no URL that resets to Schedule.
3. The Analysis entry opens round 1 and never names the Grand Prix.
4. Race Analysis and the Pit Wall are islands that duplicate each other.
5. The driver page cannot reach its team or its races.
6. The season is not shared between pages.
7. Any-lap telemetry exists only in the Pit Wall, which is hidden on phones and opens empty.
8. Four copies of the standings and three of the results.
9. Sessions are never links, and the home card hides the session's clock time.
10. Circuit and race history are dead ends.
