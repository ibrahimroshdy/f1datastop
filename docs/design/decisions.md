# F1 DataStop design release: decisions

Every design choice for this release, numbered. Later documents and PRs cite these
numbers. A changed decision gets a new row that supersedes the old one; rows are never
edited away. Audit: [`audits/ui-2026-11-10.md`](audits/ui-2026-11-10.md). Board:
`docs/design/f1datastop-design-board.html`, built by `python3 docs/design/board-src/build.py`.

| # | Decision | Lives in |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Process, copied from Mondar 0.2.** Audit first, then a concepts board, then key screens, then the build in phases (tokens and shell, components, charts, pages, brand assets), one PR each. The board is generated from source and never edited by hand. The owner picks direction, logo, name and tagline; nothing is decided for him. | `docs/design/` |
| D2 | **The audit of 2026-11-10 is the baseline.** 12 routes at 320, 375, 768 and 1280 px in dark and light. Headline findings: 97 cut names at 320 px, 64 middle dots in source, gauge bars on 7 list surfaces, 2 native `<select>`s, no list with URL-backed search and filter, sideways scroll on `/standings` at 320 px, three different reds and four `theme-color` values. | `docs/design/audits/ui-2026-11-10.md` |
| D3 | **Concepts, round 1:** A, Floodlight (a night race: warm asphalt, white light, sodium orange); B, Classification (the timing sheet set like a magazine: paper, navy ink, serif titles, vermilion only on the mark); C, Livery (aubergine-black and blush, a magenta livery that turns into the team's colour on its own pages). Each is shown night and day with contrast numbers, its type, a race card, the standings and a lap chart. The owner picks: D10. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py` |
| D4 | **Data colours never move.** Team colours from `web/src/constants/f1Colors.ts`, purple for fastest, green for personal best, tyre compound colours, and the position and status meanings are identical in every direction and theme. The brand colour is for marks, the act colour and the logo; it is never a chart series or a row fill. Every text role holds 4.5:1 on its card in both themes, checked by the board's build. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py`, later `web/src/index.css` |
| D5 | **Type roles.** One display face for page and card titles, one UI sans for everything else, one mono for every time, gap, position and points figure, always with tabular figures. Nothing under 12 px on a phone (the audit found 83 to 120 text nodes under 12 px per content page). | later `web/src/index.css` |
| D6 | **Logo, round 1.** Six marks: Apex, Pit box, Stop flag, Pit board, Speed trace, Grid slot, each one 64-unit geometry in `logos.py` shown at 16, 32 and 180 px and as a lockup, in the colours of each direction. The current roundel is not offered again: at 16 px its strokes vanish and it reads as a red dot. The name stays F1 DataStop unless the owner says otherwise. The owner picks: D11. | `docs/design/board-src/logos.py` |
| D7 | **The shell is Mondar PR #41's, at every width.** A floating header pill; a floating dock whose active tab is an act-filled capsule with the same footprint as the others; one scroll root behind both bars; a floating sidebar and top bar on tablet and desktop; opaque sheets that rise and drag to dismiss; a sliding segmented pill; View Transitions between pages; a 0.97 press. Every surface is solid, with no backdrop blur anywhere. Drawn in phase 2, built in phase 3. | later `web/src/components/layout/` |
| D8 | **House rules carried over from Mondar.** Phones never cut text to an ellipsis; no middle dots anywhere; no native `<select>`, date picker, `alert`, `confirm` or `prompt`; no gauge or progress bar on a table or list row, a whole's composition is one stacked strip; every list has search, filter, group and pagination in the URL; copy is plain, short and factual. | every phase |
| D9 | **Open, for the owner: the Cockpit theme.** `dark-glass` ("Cockpit") is a third theme built on blur and red glows, used in 41 files. D7 makes every surface solid, so it cannot stay as it is. Options: (a) keep a third theme, made solid in the new materials (a darker, higher-contrast night); (b) fold it into night and move anyone on `dark-glass` to `dark`; (c) drop the theme switch to night and day only. Not decided until he answers. | `web/src/context/ThemeContext.tsx` |
| D10 | **Open, for the owner: the direction.** After round 2 (D12) the three directions share one palette and differ in type and material. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py` |
| D11 | **Logo: Stop flag** (owner, 2026-11-10: "good start and idea Stop flag"). A stop octagon carrying the chequered flag, in F1 DataStop red. Round 2 shows it as picked and in three variations (Waving flag, Finish line, Ring); the owner picks the variation. The other five round 1 marks are retired. | `docs/design/board-src/logos.py` |
| D12 | **F1 DataStop keeps its red and carbon** (owner, 2026-11-10: "f1datastop has been iconic in its red colored and logo, also you copied over kept and mondar colors"). Supersedes D3's palettes: every direction uses the Deep Carbon ladder and one red, #E10600, in both themes, with white words on it (5.0:1). Secondary text is brightened (`#D2D3DA` and `#ABACB5` on the night card, 12.8 and 8.5:1) so nothing is grey on black. Titles take a faint red tint. One red everywhere, replacing today's three. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py`, later `web/src/index.css` |
| D13 | **What comes over from Mondar and Kept is the experience, not the colours** (owner, 2026-11-10: "what i meant is more of its ux of pwa and new webpage and details"). The PWA shell and its details from D7 and D8 carry over; Mondar's and Kept's palettes, warm stone and tints do not. | every phase |
