# F1 DataStop design release: decisions

Every design choice for this release, numbered. Later documents and PRs cite these
numbers. A changed decision gets a new row that supersedes the old one; rows are never
edited away. Audit: [`audits/ui-2026-11-10.md`](audits/ui-2026-11-10.md). Board:
`docs/design/f1datastop-design-board.html`, built by `python3 docs/design/board-src/build.py`.

| # | Decision | Lives in |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Process, copied from Mondar 0.2.** Audit first, then a concepts board, then key screens, then the build in phases (tokens and shell, components, charts, pages, brand assets), one PR each. The board is generated from source and never edited by hand. The owner picks direction, logo, name and tagline; nothing is decided for him. | `docs/design/` |
| D2 | **The audit of 2026-11-10 is the baseline.** 12 routes at 320, 375, 768 and 1280 px in dark and light. Headline findings: 97 cut names at 320 px, 64 middle dots in source, gauge bars on 7 list surfaces, 2 native `<select>`s, no list with URL-backed search and filter, sideways scroll on `/standings` at 320 px, three different reds and four `theme-color` values. | `docs/design/audits/ui-2026-11-10.md` |
| D3 | **Concepts, round 1:** A, Floodlight (a night race: warm asphalt, white light, sodium orange); B, Classification (the timing sheet set like a magazine: paper, navy ink, serif titles, vermilion only on the mark); C, Livery (aubergine-black and blush, a magenta livery that turns into the team's colour on its own pages). Each is shown night and day with contrast numbers, its type, a race card, the standings and a lap chart. The owner picks: D10. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py` |
| D4 | **Data colours never move.** Team colours from `web/src/constants/f1Colors.ts`, purple for fastest, green for personal best, tyre compound colours, and the position and status meanings are identical in every direction and theme. The brand colour is for marks, the act colour and the logo; it is never a chart series or a row fill. Every text role holds 4.5:1 on its card in both themes, checked by the board's build. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py`, later `web/src/index.css` |
| D5 | **Type roles.** One display face for page and card titles, one UI sans for everything else, one mono for every time, gap, position and points figure, always with tabular figures. Nothing under 12 px on a phone (the audit found 83 to 120 text nodes under 12 px per content page). | later `web/src/index.css` |
| D6 | **Logo, round 1.** Six marks: Apex, Pit box, Stop flag, Pit board, Speed trace, Grid slot, each one 64-unit geometry in `logos.py` shown at 16, 32 and 180 px and as a lockup, in the colours of each direction. The current roundel is not offered again: at 16 px its strokes vanish and it reads as a red dot. The name stays F1 DataStop unless the owner says otherwise. The owner picks: D11. | `docs/design/board-src/logos.py` |
| D7 | **The shell is Mondar PR #41's, at every width.** A floating header pill; a floating dock whose active tab is an act-filled capsule with the same footprint as the others; one scroll root behind both bars; a floating sidebar and top bar on tablet and desktop; opaque sheets that rise and drag to dismiss; a sliding segmented pill; View Transitions between pages; a 0.97 press. Every surface is solid, with no backdrop blur anywhere. Drawn in phase 2, built in phase 3. | later `web/src/components/layout/` |
| D8 | **House rules carried over from Mondar.** Phones never cut text to an ellipsis; no middle dots anywhere; no native `<select>`, date picker, `alert`, `confirm` or `prompt`; no gauge or progress bar on a table or list row, a whole's composition is one stacked strip; every list has search, filter, group and pagination in the URL; copy is plain, short and factual. | every phase |
| D9 | **Open, for the owner: the Cockpit theme.** `dark-glass` ("Cockpit") is a third theme built on blur and red glows, used in 41 files. D7 makes every surface solid, so it cannot stay as it is. Options: (a) keep a third theme, made solid in the new materials (a darker, higher-contrast night); (b) fold it into night and move anyone on `dark-glass` to `dark`; (c) drop the theme switch to night and day only. Not decided until he answers. | `web/src/context/ThemeContext.tsx` |
| D10 | **Open, for the owner: the direction.** After round 2 (D12) the three directions share one palette and differ in type and material. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py` |
| D11 | **Logo: Stop flag** (owner, 2026-11-10: "good start and idea Stop flag"). A stop octagon carrying the chequered flag, in F1 DataStop red. Round 2 shows it as picked and in three variations (Waving flag, Finish line, Ring); the owner picks the variation. The other five round 1 marks are retired. | `docs/design/board-src/logos.py` |
| D12 | **F1 DataStop keeps its red and carbon** (owner, 2026-11-10: "f1datastop has been iconic in its red colored and logo, also you copied over kept and mondar colors"). Supersedes D3's palettes: every direction uses the Deep Carbon ladder and one red, #E10600, in both themes, with white words on it (5.0:1). Secondary text is brightened (`#D2D3DA` and `#ABACB5` on the night card, 12.8 and 8.5:1) so nothing is grey on black. Titles take a faint red tint. One red everywhere, replacing today's three. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py`, later `web/src/index.css` |
| D13 | **What comes over from Mondar and Kept is the experience, not the colours** (owner, 2026-11-10: "what i meant is more of its ux of pwa and new webpage and details"). The PWA shell and its details from D7 and D8 carry over; Mondar's and Kept's palettes, warm stone and tints do not. | every phase |
| D14 | **The logo stays the rear wing, made better** (owner, 2026-11-10: "don't need a revamp of logo but need the existing one better, same idea its the rear wing of a f1 car"). Supersedes D6 and D11: no new mark. Today's `logo.svg` (an F1 rear wing seen from behind on a red disc) is redrawn flat in round 3, with parts thick enough for 16 px: Refined, Bold, Open flap, Square. The owner picks the version. | `docs/design/board-src/wing.py`, later `logo.svg`, `web/public/` |
| D15 | **A race is its Grand Prix and its flag, never a round code** (owner, 2026-11-10: "stress on prix not be R1..R2.. and be flags"). Lists, cards, pickers and headers lead with the country flag and the Grand Prix name ("Las Vegas Grand Prix"); "R21" and "Round 21" go from titles and rows. Flags come from flag-icons (already used through `react-country-flag`), 4:3, rounded 3 px, with a hairline so white flags hold on white. | `docs/design/board-src/race_bits.py`, later `web/src/components/common/CountryFlag.tsx` |
| D16 | **One tyre icon and one tyre colour set** (owner, 2026-11-10: "perfected tyres and their icons"). Pirelli-style: the black tyre, the compound's band near the rim, the letter in the band's colour, the same drawing from 16 to 48 px. Colours are the social cards' Pirelli set (soft `#DA291C`, medium `#FFD12E`, hard `#F0F0EC`, intermediate `#43B02A`, wet `#2F8CFF`), replacing the pit wall's separate `TYRE_HSL`. A stint is one strip summing to the race distance, with its laps in words. | `docs/design/board-src/race_bits.py`, later `TyreBadge.tsx`, `replayEngine.ts` |
| D17 | **Direction: Floodlight Livery** (owner, 2026-11-10: "i need a mix of floodlight and C, Livery", "font wise floodlight is much better"). Settles D10. Floodlight's type (Saira Condensed titles in capitals, Saira UI, JetBrains Mono figures) and its light from the top of the page; Livery's diagonal stripe on the one lead card of a page, red by default and the team's colour on a team or driver page; D12's red and carbon; cards 20 px round. The stripe never sits on list rows and never carries data. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py`, later `web/src/index.css` |
| D18 | **The livery stripe carries the page's identity** (owner, 2026-11-10: "can that change by team color or driver color with number on it and sometimes fits a country flag too?"). On a driver page it is the team colour with the car number; on a team page the team colour with both car numbers; on a Grand Prix the host country's flag; elsewhere F1 DataStop red. The number is set in Saira Condensed italic in whichever of white or carbon reads best on the colour. Decoration only: the same facts are in the card's text. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py` (`livery`) |
| D19 | **Proposed, waiting for the owner: every name is a link.** Drivers, teams, Grands Prix, circuits and sessions, everywhere they appear: rows, cards, charts' legends, the Pit Wall. From the flow review. | `docs/design/audits/flows-2026-11-10.md` |
| D20 | **Proposed, waiting for the owner: a Grand Prix is a page.** It replaces the race dialog and Race Analysis, with tabs for Schedule, Results, Laps, Positions, Strategy, Telemetry, Replay, Weather and Radio; the dialog goes. From the flow review. | `docs/design/audits/flows-2026-11-10.md` |
| D21 | **Proposed, waiting for the owner: one season, in the URL, kept on every page.** Picked in the header pill; Standings, profiles and Home follow it. From the flow review. | `docs/design/audits/flows-2026-11-10.md` |
| D22 | **Proposed, waiting for the owner: navigation by the nouns.** Dock: Home, Races, Standings, Drivers, More (Teams, Circuits, Pit Wall, Docs). Sidebar: all seven. Search in the header pill finds any driver, team, Grand Prix or circuit. From the flow review. | `docs/design/audits/flows-2026-11-10.md` |
| D23 | **Proposed, waiting for the owner: no content behind a dialog without a URL.** Sheets and dialogs are for pickers, filters and confirmations. One renderer per thing: results, standings and the Sankey each exist once. From the flow review. | `docs/design/audits/flows-2026-11-10.md` |
| D24 | **Proposed, waiting for the owner: entries land on now.** Races, a Grand Prix and the Pit Wall open on the next or latest race, never round 1 or an empty state. From the flow review. | `docs/design/audits/flows-2026-11-10.md` |
| D25 | **The livery stripe is colour only** (owner, 2026-11-10: "the numbers and flag was really bad idea. ignore it. colors only is good"). Supersedes D18: F1 DataStop red on most pages, the team's colour on a team or driver page; no car numbers, no flags in the stripe. | `docs/design/board-src/concepts.py` (`livery`) |
| D26 | **Drivers show their face** (owner, 2026-11-10: "why don't you show drivers avatars"). Every place a driver is listed (standings, results, team pages, stints, the driver page header) shows the F1 headshot the app already uses (`buildF1DriverHeadshotUrl` in `web/src/constants/f1DriverPortraitMedia.ts`), round, ringed in the team colour: 40 px in rows, 28 px in compact rows, 72 px on the driver page. Initials when there is no headshot. | `docs/design/board-src/avatars.py`, later `web/src/components/f1/F1Avatar.tsx` |
| D27 | **The connected structure is accepted** (owner, 2026-11-10: "Yes, draw screens", in answer to "OK the connected structure so I can draw the screens?"). D19 to D24 stand as written. Key screens are drawn on it before any build (Mondar's D19 process): Home, a Grand Prix, laps and telemetry, Standings, Races, a driver page, and a sheet, at phone and desktop, night and day. | `docs/design/screens/`, `docs/design/f1datastop-screens.html` |
| D28 | **The logo stays as it is** (owner, 2026-11-10: "my logo is much better"). Supersedes D14's redraw round: today's `logo.svg`, the rear wing on the red disc, is the mark, unchanged. The screens use it. Phase 7 only exports it to the icon sizes. | `logo.svg`, `docs/design/screens/shell.py` |
| D29 | **One compact row, one height** (owner, 2026-11-10: "this section is not uniform so bloated", on Home's last race and drivers cards). Cards on Home and other summaries use one row: position, a 32 px face, the surname over the team, one figure; every row the same height, nothing wraps to three lines. Full names stay on the full pages and tables. | `docs/design/screens/content.py` (`crow`) |
| D30 | **The qualifying to race Sankey comes back, and a race opens a quick look first** (owner, 2026-11-10: "the qualifying to race sankey is everyone's favorite where is it", "where are the grand prix detailed page.. with pop up"). The Sankey sits on Home under the last race and on the Grand Prix page beside the results; places gained or lost are written beside each finish. Tapping a race anywhere opens a quick look (a sheet on a phone, a dialog on a desktop) with winner, pole, fastest lap, the Sankey and Open Grand Prix. Amends D23: the quick look has its own address (`?race=`), so Back closes it and a link opens it. | `docs/design/screens/content.py`, `charts.py` |
