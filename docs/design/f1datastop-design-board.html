Watch for: The stripe is decoration. It stays on the one lead card of a page, never on list rows, and never carries data meaning.

How it connects

You said the app makes you click a lot and things feel displaced. This is why, page by page, and the structure that fixes it. The full review is docs/design/audits/flows-2026-11-10.md.

What is wrong today

  1. Driver names are links in 2 of about 24 places; teams in 3 of about 17; Grands Prix in 2; sessions in none.
  2. Every race click on Home and the Calendar opens a dialog with no URL, which always opens on Schedule. It is the only way into Race Analysis.
  3. Race Analysis and the Pit Wall link out to nothing and duplicate each other: two lap charts, two telemetry views, two replays.
  4. The Analysis tab opens round 1, not the latest race; the Pit Wall opens empty and is hidden on phones.
  5. Each page picks its season alone; five pages are locked to this year.
  6. Four copies of the standings, three of the race results.

The proposed structure

Pages are the nouns of the sport. Each one links to every other noun it mentions.

Home

Next Grand Prix with its sessions in your time, last Grand Prix with its podium, both standings tops. Every card opens a page.

Races

Every Grand Prix of the season, flag first, grouped by month, searchable. The default lands on the next or latest one.

Grand Prix

One page per weekend. Tabs: Schedule, Results (one session switch: practice, sprint, qualifying, race), Laps, Positions, Strategy (tyres and pit stops), Telemetry (any lap), Replay, Weather, Radio. Replaces the race dialog and Race Analysis; old links redirect.

Pit Wall

The full-screen engineer view of the same weekend. Opened from a Grand Prix with that race loaded, and links back. Opens on the latest race, never empty.

Standings

Drivers and teams for the season in the header; one renderer, used again by Home.

Drivers

List with search, team filter and season; each driver page has the team, every race result as a link, and head to head.

Teams

List and team pages; drivers link to their pages.

Circuits

A list (new) and circuit pages; past editions and winners link to their Grand Prix and drivers.

Proposed, for your OK

D19Every name is a link. Drivers, teams, Grands Prix, circuits and sessions, everywhere they appear: rows, cards, charts' legends, the Pit Wall.
D20A Grand Prix is a page. It replaces the race dialog and Race Analysis, with tabs for Schedule, Results, Laps, Positions, Strategy, Telemetry, Replay, Weather and Radio; the dialog goes.
D21One season, in the URL, kept on every page. Picked in the header pill; Standings, profiles and Home follow it.
D22Navigation by the nouns. Dock: Home, Races, Standings, Drivers, More (Teams, Circuits, Pit Wall, Docs). Sidebar: all seven. Search in the header pill finds any driver, team, Grand Prix or circuit.
D23No content behind a dialog without a URL. Sheets and dialogs are for pickers, filters and confirmations. One renderer per thing: results, standings and the Sankey each exist once.
D24Entries land on now. Races, a Grand Prix and the Pit Wall open on the next or latest race, never round 1 or an empty state.

Grands Prix and tyres

A race is its Grand Prix and its flag, never "R21". Every list, card and picker leads with the flag and the Grand Prix name; the specimens above show the race card and a calendar list that way. Tyres are one Pirelli-style icon at every size: the black tyre, the compound's band near the rim, the letter in the band's colour, in one colour set shared by the app, the charts and the social cards. A stint is one strip summing to the race, with its words beside it.

Your picks

Nothing is built until you choose. The answers go into docs/design/decisions.md.

  1. The structure in "How it connects", D19 to D24: OK as proposed, or what to change.
  2. The Cockpit theme (D9): it is the glass one. Keep it as a third, solid theme in the new materials, fold it into night, or drop it?
  3. Type: keep the direction's pairing, or bring it to a specimen of three like Mondar's.

Decisions so far

D1Process, copied from Mondar 0.2. Audit first, then a concepts board, then key screens, then the build in phases (tokens and shell, components, charts, pages, brand assets), one PR each. The board is generated from source and never edited by hand. The owner picks direction, logo, name and tagline; nothing is decided for him.
D2The audit of 2026-11-10 is the baseline. 12 routes at 320, 375, 768 and 1280 px in dark and light. Headline findings: 97 cut names at 320 px, 64 middle dots in source, gauge bars on 7 list surfaces, 2 native <select>s, no list with URL-backed search and filter, sideways scroll on /standings at 320 px, three different reds and four theme-color values.
D3Concepts, round 1: A, Floodlight (a night race: warm asphalt, white light, sodium orange); B, Classification (the timing sheet set like a magazine: paper, navy ink, serif titles, vermilion only on the mark); C, Livery (aubergine-black and blush, a magenta livery that turns into the team's colour on its own pages). Each is shown night and day with contrast numbers, its type, a race card, the standings and a lap chart. The owner picks: D10.
D4Data colours never move. Team colours from web/src/constants/f1Colors.ts, purple for fastest, green for personal best, tyre compound colours, and the position and status meanings are identical in every direction and theme. The brand colour is for marks, the act colour and the logo; it is never a chart series or a row fill. Every text role holds 4.5:1 on its card in both themes, checked by the board's build.
D5Type roles. One display face for page and card titles, one UI sans for everything else, one mono for every time, gap, position and points figure, always with tabular figures. Nothing under 12 px on a phone (the audit found 83 to 120 text nodes under 12 px per content page).
D6Logo, round 1. Six marks: Apex, Pit box, Stop flag, Pit board, Speed trace, Grid slot, each one 64-unit geometry in logos.py shown at 16, 32 and 180 px and as a lockup, in the colours of each direction. The current roundel is not offered again: at 16 px its strokes vanish and it reads as a red dot. The name stays F1 DataStop unless the owner says otherwise. The owner picks: D11.
D7The shell is Mondar PR #41's, at every width. A floating header pill; a floating dock whose active tab is an act-filled capsule with the same footprint as the others; one scroll root behind both bars; a floating sidebar and top bar on tablet and desktop; opaque sheets that rise and drag to dismiss; a sliding segmented pill; View Transitions between pages; a 0.97 press. Every surface is solid, with no backdrop blur anywhere. Drawn in phase 2, built in phase 3.
D8House rules carried over from Mondar. Phones never cut text to an ellipsis; no middle dots anywhere; no native <select>, date picker, alert, confirm or prompt; no gauge or progress bar on a table or list row, a whole's composition is one stacked strip; every list has search, filter, group and pagination in the URL; copy is plain, short and factual.
D9Open, for the owner: the Cockpit theme. dark-glass ("Cockpit") is a third theme built on blur and red glows, used in 41 files. D7 makes every surface solid, so it cannot stay as it is. Options: (a) keep a third theme, made solid in the new materials (a darker, higher-contrast night); (b) fold it into night and move anyone on dark-glass to dark; (c) drop the theme switch to night and day only. Not decided until he answers.
D10Open, for the owner: the direction. After round 2 (D12) the three directions share one palette and differ in type and material.
