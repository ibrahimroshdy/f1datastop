F1 DataStop design release, round 1, 10 Nov 2026

Three directions and six marks

Pick one direction and one mark, or mix: a mark from one list can wear another direction's colours. Each direction is shown in its own night and day, with the same three pieces: a race card, the standings and a lap chart. Figures are shaped like the real season; session times are sample data. Team colours, purple for fastest and tyre colours are the real ones and never change.

Your picks, and one question

Nothing is built until you choose. The answers go into docs/design/decisions.md as D10 onwards.

  1. Direction: A Floodlight, B Classification, C Livery, or a mix (say which parts).
  2. Mark: Apex, Pit box, Stop flag, Pit board, Speed trace or Grid slot, or none and a second round.
  3. The Cockpit theme (D9): it is the glass one. Keep it as a third, solid theme in the new materials, fold it into night, or drop it?
  4. Type: keep the direction's pairing, or bring it to a specimen of three like Mondar's.

Decisions so far

D1Process, copied from Mondar 0.2. Audit first, then a concepts board, then key screens, then the build in phases (tokens and shell, components, charts, pages, brand assets), one PR each. The board is generated from source and never edited by hand. The owner picks direction, logo, name and tagline; nothing is decided for him.
D2The audit of 2026-11-10 is the baseline. 12 routes at 320, 375, 768 and 1280 px in dark and light. Headline findings: 97 cut names at 320 px, 64 middle dots in source, gauge bars on 7 list surfaces, 2 native <select>s, no list with URL-backed search and filter, sideways scroll on /standings at 320 px, three different reds and four theme-color values.
D3Concepts, round 1: A, Floodlight (a night race: warm asphalt, white light, sodium orange); B, Classification (the timing sheet set like a magazine: paper, navy ink, serif titles, vermilion only on the mark); C, Livery (aubergine-black and blush, a magenta livery that turns into the team's colour on its own pages). Each is shown night and day with contrast numbers, its type, a race card, the standings and a lap chart. The owner picks: D10.
D4Data colours never move. Team colours from web/src/constants/f1Colors.ts, purple for fastest, green for personal best, tyre compound colours, and the position and status meanings are identical in every direction and theme. The brand colour is for marks, the act colour and the logo; it is never a chart series or a row fill. Every text role holds 4.5:1 on its card in both themes, checked by the board's build.
D5Type roles. One display face for page and card titles, one UI sans for everything else, one mono for every time, gap, position and points figure, always with tabular figures. Nothing under 12 px on a phone (the audit found 83 to 120 text nodes under 12 px per content page).
D6Logo, round 1. Six marks: Apex, Pit box, Stop flag, Pit board, Speed trace, Grid slot, each one 64-unit geometry in logos.py shown at 16, 32 and 180 px and as a lockup, in the colours of each direction. The current roundel is not offered again: at 16 px its strokes vanish and it reads as a red dot. The name stays F1 DataStop unless the owner says otherwise. The owner picks: D11.
D7The shell is Mondar PR #41's, at every width. A floating header pill; a floating dock whose active tab is an act-filled capsule with the same footprint as the others; one scroll root behind both bars; a floating sidebar and top bar on tablet and desktop; opaque sheets that rise and drag to dismiss; a sliding segmented pill; View Transitions between pages; a 0.97 press. Every surface is solid, with no backdrop blur anywhere. Drawn in phase 2, built in phase 3.
D8House rules carried over from Mondar. Phones never cut text to an ellipsis; no middle dots anywhere; no native <select>, date picker, alert, confirm or prompt; no gauge or progress bar on a table or list row, a whole's composition is one stacked strip; every list has search, filter, group and pagination in the URL; copy is plain, short and factual.
D9Open, for the owner: the Cockpit theme. dark-glass ("Cockpit") is a third theme built on blur and red glows, used in 41 files. D7 makes every surface solid, so it cannot stay as it is. Options: (a) keep a third theme, made solid in the new materials (a darker, higher-contrast night); (b) fold it into night and move anyone on dark-glass to dark; (c) drop the theme switch to night and day only. Not decided until he answers.
